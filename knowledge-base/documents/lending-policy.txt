
LENDING POLICY — SYNTHETIC REFERENCE DOCUMENT
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Enterprise GenAI Platform technical demonstration

IMPORTANT:
This document contains fictional lending rules created exclusively
for software testing. It is not a real financial institution's policy.

1. APPLICATION ELIGIBILITY

Applicants must provide valid income information, current debt
obligations, requested loan amount, and repayment history.

A credit score may be provided as additional supporting information.

2. DEBT-TO-INCOME RATIO

Debt-to-income ratio (DTI) is calculated as:

Monthly debt obligations / Monthly income × 100

DTI below 35%:
Eligible for standard underwriting review.

DTI from 35% to 50%:
Requires enhanced manual review.

DTI above 50%:
Requires escalation to a senior credit reviewer.

3. MISSED PAYMENT RULES

Zero missed payments:
No additional review required based solely on payment history.

One missed payment:
Flag for additional repayment-history verification.

Two or more missed payments:
Enhanced manual review is mandatory.

4. CREDIT SCORE

Credit score of 750 or higher:
Strong credit profile indicator.

Credit score from 650 to 749:
Standard credit assessment.

Credit score below 650:
Additional risk assessment required.

A credit score alone must not determine approval or rejection.

5. LOAN APPROVAL

The AI assistant must not approve or reject loan applications.

Final lending decisions require authorized human review.

6. INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION

If policy documentation does not provide sufficient information
to answer a question, the assistant must state that the available
policy information is insufficient.

The assistant must not invent lending rules.
